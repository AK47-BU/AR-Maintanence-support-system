AR Marker Reference

Use these markers with the AR Maintenance System. Display on a screen or print and place at inspection locations.

How to use: Open this page on a laptop or tablet. Open the scanner on your phone. Point the phone camera at the marker on this screen. The AR overlay will appear on the phone. Keep the marker flat, well-lit, and at least 15 cm wide for reliable detection.

Each mode uses a different marker so both apps can run independently without triggering each other. Use the Hiro marker for the Fault Scanner, and the Kanji marker for the Tool Check.
Printable versions: Search for "AR.js hiro marker" and "AR.js kanji marker" — the standard images are freely available. Print at A5 or larger and place face-up on a flat surface. For best results use matte paper (glossy causes reflection artefacts).
